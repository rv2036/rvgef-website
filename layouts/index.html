Hong Kong is a vibrant international city and major business hub in Asia, known for:

  • Strategic location in the Asia-Pacific region

  • Diverse culinary experiences

Transportation

From Hong Kong International Airport

Airport Express + MTR:

  • Take Airport Express to Hong Kong Station (24 minutes)
  • Transfer to MTR Island Line to Wan Chai Station (5 minutes)
  • Walk to HKCEC (5 minutes)

Taxi: Approximately 30-40 minutes (HK$300-400)

Getting to the Venue

MTR (Metro):

  • Wan Chai Station (Island Line) - 5 minutes walk
  • Exhibition Centre Station (not yet open)

Tram: Alight at Convention Avenue

Bus: Multiple routes serve the area

Local Transportation Tips
  • Consider getting an Octopus Card for convenient payment on all public transport
  • Hong Kong's public transportation is efficient, affordable, and English-friendly
  • Taxis are readily available and reasonably priced
  • Most areas are pedestrian-friendly with excellent signage
